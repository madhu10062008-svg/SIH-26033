# SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

# Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

# Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

# Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

# Proposed Solution

1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

We propose FarmDirect, a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, shops, restaurants, and bulk buyers. Farmers can list their products, set prices, and receive orders without depending on multiple intermediaries. The platform also helps them find transport services and sell their produce more easily.

2. How it addresses the problem

By connecting farmers and buyers directly, the platform reduces unnecessary middlemen and helps farmers earn more from their produce. Consumers can also buy fresh products at more reasonable prices. The platform makes selling, purchasing, and delivery simpler.

3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

FarmDirect combines direct selling, delivery support, and AI-based demand prediction in one platform. It helps farmers understand market demand, avoid selling excess produce, and find suitable buyers. Our focus is on making the platform affordable, simple, and accessible to farmers.

# Technical Approach

1. Technologies to be used (e.g. programming languages, frameworks, hardware)

• Frontend: React.js
• Backend: Python (FastAPI)
• Database: PostgreSQL
• AI: Python and Scikit-learn for demand forecasting.
• Maps: OpenStreetMap for location and route planning.
• Payments: UPI integration.
• Deployment: Cloud hosting.

2. Methodology and process for implementation (Flow Charts/Images/ working prototype)

Farmers register and upload their products with prices and quantities. Buyers browse the listings and place orders directly. The system uses available market and order data to predict demand. Once an order is placed, the platform helps arrange transportation and suggests efficient delivery routes. Buyers can track orders and provide feedback after delivery.

# Feasibility and Viability

1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea

The platform can be developed using existing web technologies, smartphones, digital payment systems, and mapping services. A basic prototype with product listings, orders, and farmer-buyer connections can be built first, followed by AI and logistics features.

2. Potential challenges and risks

Limited internet access, lack of digital familiarity among farmers, transportation costs, and maintaining product freshness are some of the challenges.

3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges

We plan to provide a simple interface with regional language support, work with FPOs, allow shared transportation, and use transparent pricing and secure payments to build trust.

# Impact and Benefits

1. Potential impact on the target audience

The platform can help farmers reach more buyers, improve their earnings, and reduce dependency on middlemen. Consumers, retailers, and restaurants can access fresh produce at better prices, while local transport providers can gain additional business.

2. Benefits of the solution (social, economic, environmental, etc.)

• Economic: Better earnings for farmers and affordable prices for consumers.
• Social: Supports rural communities and small-scale farmers.
• Environmental: Reduces food wastage and unnecessary transportation.
• Supply Chain: Makes agricultural trading simpler and more transparent.
